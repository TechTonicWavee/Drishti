PRESSURE VESSEL INSPECTION GUIDELINES
Document reference: SAMPLE-SOP-MECH-007, Revision 2

1. SCOPE
These guidelines cover in-service inspection of pressure vessels in refinery
service, including separators, knockout drums, reactors and storage bullets.
They do not cover fired heaters, piping, or atmospheric storage tanks, each of
which is governed by its own procedure.

2. INSPECTION INTERVALS
2.1 External visual inspection shall be carried out at intervals not
exceeding five years, or at one quarter of the calculated remaining life,
whichever is shorter.
2.2 Internal inspection shall be carried out at intervals not exceeding ten
years, or at one half of the calculated remaining life, whichever is shorter.
2.3 Where a vessel is in severe cyclic or corrosive service, the Inspection
Engineer shall shorten these intervals and record the justification in the
vessel file.
2.4 An interval may never be extended without a documented fitness-for-service
assessment approved by the Chief Inspector.

3. EXTERNAL VISUAL INSPECTION
3.1 Examine the shell, heads, nozzles and reinforcing pads for corrosion,
bulging, mechanical damage and coating breakdown.
3.2 Inspect supports, saddles and anchor bolts. Pay particular attention to
corrosion under insulation at support contact points, which is the most common
location for undetected wall loss.
3.3 Examine all attached small-bore connections and vents. These fail more
often than the vessel shell itself.
3.4 Record findings with photographs and mark any suspect areas for thickness
measurement.

4. THICKNESS MEASUREMENT
4.1 Take ultrasonic thickness readings at established condition monitoring
locations. Use the same locations at each inspection so that corrosion rates
can be trended meaningfully.
4.2 A minimum of four readings shall be taken per shell course, at the four
quadrant positions.
4.3 Calculate the corrosion rate from the current and previous readings, and
recalculate remaining life. If measured thickness is below the minimum
required thickness, the vessel shall be taken out of service immediately and
referred to the Chief Inspector.

5. INTERNAL INSPECTION
5.1 Internal inspection requires the vessel to be isolated, drained, purged
and gas tested, and a confined space entry permit to be in force.
5.2 Examine internal surfaces for pitting, cracking, erosion and damage to
internals such as trays, demister pads and distributors.
5.3 Where stress corrosion cracking is suspected, apply wet fluorescent
magnetic particle testing to welds and heat affected zones.

6. RECORDS
6.1 All findings shall be entered into the vessel file within ten working
days of the inspection.
6.2 The vessel file shall contain the inspection history, thickness trend
data, calculated remaining life, and the date of the next required inspection.
