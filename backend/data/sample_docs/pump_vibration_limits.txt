ROTATING EQUIPMENT VIBRATION LIMITS
Document reference: SAMPLE-SOP-ROT-031, Revision 4

1. SCOPE
Applies to all centrifugal pumps and compressors above 15 kW in refinery
service.

2. ALARM AND TRIP THRESHOLDS
2.1 Overall vibration velocity shall be trended weekly at the bearing housings.
2.2 The alert threshold is 4.5 mm/s RMS. Above this, increase monitoring to
daily and raise a maintenance notification.
2.3 The alarm threshold is 7.1 mm/s RMS. Above this, plan a shutdown within
seven days.
2.4 The trip threshold is 11.0 mm/s RMS. Above this, stop the machine
immediately and switch to the standby unit.

3. BEARING TEMPERATURE
3.1 Bearing housing temperature shall not exceed 85 degrees Celsius.
3.2 A rise of more than 10 degrees within one shift is itself a finding, even
if the absolute value remains acceptable.
