FLARE SYSTEM OPERATION AND MONITORING
Document reference: SAMPLE-SOP-FLARE-009, Revision 1

1. PURPOSE
This procedure covers routine operation and monitoring of the elevated flare
serving Units 1 through 4.

2. PILOT INTEGRITY
2.1 All three pilots shall be confirmed lit at the start of every shift by
thermocouple reading, not by visual inspection alone.
2.2 A pilot thermocouple reading below 250 degrees Celsius shall be treated as
a failed pilot and reported immediately.
2.3 Never isolate more than one pilot fuel line at a time.

3. SMOKE AND STEAM
3.1 Maintain smokeless operation using assist steam. Target a steam-to-relief
mass ratio of 0.3 to 0.5.
3.2 Excess steam causes flame instability and wastes energy. Above a ratio of
0.7, reduce assist steam in 10 percent increments.

4. LIQUID CARRYOVER
4.1 The knockout drum level shall not exceed 60 percent. Above this, liquid
carryover to the flare tip becomes likely and can cause burning rain.
4.2 Pump out to slops if the level reaches 55 percent.

5. RECORDING
5.1 Log flare gas flow, steam flow and knockout drum level hourly.
5.2 Any flaring event exceeding 30 minutes shall be reported to the
Environment cell within one hour.
